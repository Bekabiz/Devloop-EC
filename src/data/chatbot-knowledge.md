## Services

1. Design & Study (Μελέτη): architectural and structural studies for residential, commercial, tourism and industrial projects. Complete engineering solutions from concept through detailed plans.
2. Licensing & Coordination (Αδειοδότηση): full building permit management. Feasibility studies. Integration of projects into EU and national development funding programmes (including ΕΣΠΑ).
3. Construction & Supervision (Κατασκευή): reinforced concrete (οπλισμένο σκυρόδεμα) and steel/metal construction, renovation, repair and structural reinforcement. End-to-end project management from foundations to handover.

Full pipeline offered: finding the property → design → financing → project management → construction → delivery.

## Regions served

Ilia (Ηλεία), Attica (Αττική), the Cyclades (Κυκλάδες), Thessaly (Θεσσαλία), Messinia (Μεσσηνία), and the Ionian Islands (Ιόνια Νησιά).

## Project types

Residential buildings and housing complexes, tourist accommodation and facilities, commercial and industrial units, sports facilities, and public infrastructure. Also renovations, repairs and structural reinforcement of existing buildings.

## Common questions — answer confidently

- Do you handle building permits? Yes, full licensing and permit management is one of the three core services.
- Do you help with EU or ΕΣΠΑ funding? Yes: feasibility studies and integration into EU and national development funding programmes.
- Do you only work in Ilia? No: six regions — Ilia, Attica, Cyclades, Thessaly, Messinia, Ionian Islands.
- Can you help me find land? Yes, the service runs from finding the property all the way through to handover.
- Do you do both design and construction? Yes. The engineering office handles studies and licensing; the construction company builds.
- Do you do renovations? Yes, including repairs and structural reinforcement of existing buildings.
- How much does it cost / how long will it take? Never answer with figures. Every project differs. Direct them to contact us for an assessment.
